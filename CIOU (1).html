How Ciof works

For customers and for workers.

  • 1
    CIOU is a marketplace connecting people who need house cleaning with people who want to earn money cleaning. Post a job, get offers, pick your cleaner — simple and secure.
  • 2
    Customers post a cleaning job. Workers apply by sending a price offer. Customer picks the best offer.
  • 3
    When a worker is chosen, the customer pays into secure escrow. The worker receives nothing until the job is confirmed complete.
  • 4
    CIOU takes a fee from workers only — 15% new, 12% at 4.3★+, 8% at 4.7★+, 5% at perfect 5.0★. Customers pay nothing extra.
  • 5
    If the job is done badly and the customer proves it, CIOU deducts from the worker and refunds the customer. Both sides always heard first.

Rules & Money Protection

Written plainly.

  • 1
    Worker fees: New workers 15%. Rating 4.3★+ = 12%. Rating 4.7★+ = 8%. Perfect 5.0★ = 5%. Customers pay no platform fee — ever.
  • 2
    Escrow: Full payment locked until job confirmed complete.
  • 3
    Dispute (customer): Open within 24 hours with photo or video proof. No proof = rejected.
  • 4
    Penalties: Minor 10–30%, Major 30–70%, Complete failure up to 100%. Repeated violations = suspended.
  • 5
    Refund: Deducted amount goes back to you minus €2 flat review fee. CIOU keeps nothing.
  • 6
    False complaints: Three false disputes = account suspended.
  • 7
    Worker rights: Workers respond with evidence. Both sides heard before any decision. Always photograph before & after every job — it's your best protection.
  • 8
    Suspension: 3+ confirmed bad complaints in 90 days = auto-suspended. One appeal allowed.
  • 9
    Payout: Within 24h of confirmation, or automatically after 72h if customer doesn't respond.
  • 10
    Taxes: Workers are self-employed. Over €520/month → register as Kleinunternehmer. CIOU is not your employer. Ask Finanzamt if unsure.
  • 11
    Worker verification: We keep it simple — just basic identity verification. No professional certifications required. If you can do the job well, you're welcome on CIOU.
  • 12
    False job completion: If a worker marks a job as complete but the customer proves it wasn't done and CIOU decides a 100% salary deduction — the worker must additionally pay 5% of the job value to CIOU and 5% to the customer. This rule applies only in 100% deduction cases.
